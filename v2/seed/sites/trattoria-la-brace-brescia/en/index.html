Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book a table?

We recommend booking, especially on Fridays, Saturdays and holidays: call us at +39 030 5550003 and we'll find you a table. On weekday lunches you'll almost always find a seat without booking.

What are your opening hours?

We're open mar-dom 12:00-14:30; ven-sab 19:00-22:30. The complete schedule with lunch and dinner shifts is in the Opening hours section of this page.

Where exactly are you located?

You'll find us at Corso Esempio 13, 25100 Brescia, 25100 Brescia. The interactive map with directions is in the Find us section of this page.

How can I contact you?

The fastest way is calling +39 030 5550003, especially for bookings and takeaway orders. Prefer writing? Use the form in the Contact section or email info@trattoria-la-brace-brescia.example.it: we reply within a few hours during opening times.

Do you offer takeaway or delivery?

Yes: call +39 030 5550003 and your order will be ready when you arrive, or arrange delivery in the Brescia area with us.

Is there parking?

You can usually find parking within a short walk of the entrance: the map with directions is in the Find us section of this page.

Do you have gluten-free options?

We can prepare some gluten-free dishes on request: tell us when ordering and we'll check with the kitchen.

Do you accept card payments?

Yes, we accept major credit and debit cards, as well as cash.
